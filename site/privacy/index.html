Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NexGen Binary LLC (“NexGen Binary,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, and protects information submitted through this website, including contact form inquiries and consultation booking requests.

This website is intended for business inquiries related to managed IT, cybersecurity, Microsoft 365, networking, cloud, and support services for dental and healthcare offices.

Information we collect

We may collect the following information when you use this website:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Practice or organization name
  • Message details you choose to submit
  • Appointment and scheduling details submitted through our booking page
  • Technical information needed for spam prevention, security, and website operations

How we use information

We use submitted information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requested consultations
  • Communicate about services, availability, and next steps
  • Schedule and manage consultation appointments
  • Protect the website against spam, abuse, and fraudulent submissions
  • Maintain and improve website performance and service delivery

Booking and third-party services

Our website may use third-party tools to operate certain features. These may include a website form processing provider, a scheduling platform for consultations, and spam-prevention or human-verification services such as hCaptcha.

When you use those features, the information you submit may be processed through those third-party services as necessary to complete your request.

What not to send through this website

Please do not submit patient records, treatment details, insurance information, Social Security numbers, payment card numbers, or other protected health information (“PHI”) through this public website, contact form, or booking notes.

If secure document exchange is needed during a client engagement, we will provide an appropriate business-approved method separately.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information submitted through this website. We may share information only as reasonably necessary to operate the website, process submissions, schedule appointments, communicate with you, comply with legal obligations, or protect our business and users.

Data retention and security

We retain submitted information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, manage consultations, maintain business records, and meet legal or operational obligations.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information, but no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

You may choose not to provide information through this website, but some features may not work without it. If you would like us to update or remove a prior website inquiry where reasonably possible, contact us using the information below.

Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
