Privacy Policy

How NexGen Binary handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: July 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NexGen Binary LLC (“NexGen Binary,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, and protects information submitted through this website.

This website is intended for business inquiries related to managed IT, cybersecurity, Microsoft 365, networking, cloud, and support services for dental and select healthcare organizations.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, phone number, practice or organization name, and message details. The current contact form prepares an email in your default mail application; the website does not confirm delivery or store the message itself.

We may also receive limited technical information needed for security, spam prevention, and normal website operation.

How We Use Information

  • Respond to inquiries and consultation requests
  • Communicate about services, availability, and next steps
  • Protect the website against spam, abuse, and fraudulent activity
  • Maintain and improve website performance and service delivery

Third-Party Services

This website may link to third-party services, including email applications, scheduling platforms, Google services, and other external websites. Information you submit directly to those providers is processed under their own terms and privacy practices.

What Not to Send

Do not submit patient records, treatment details, insurance information, Social Security numbers, payment-card information, or other protected health information (“PHI”) through this public website, email form, or consultation notes.

If secure document exchange is required during a client engagement, NexGen Binary will provide an appropriate business-approved method separately.

How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information submitted through this website. We may share information only as reasonably necessary to respond to an inquiry, operate the website, comply with legal obligations, or protect our business and users.

Data Retention and Security

We retain business communications for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, maintain records, and meet legal or operational obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may choose not to provide information through this website. To request an update or deletion of a prior website inquiry where reasonably possible, contact us at info@nexgenbinary.com.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
